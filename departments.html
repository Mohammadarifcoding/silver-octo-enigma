Diploma in Engineering

Nine technologies. One workshop-first approach.

Every programme runs for four years (eight semesters) under the Bangladesh Technical Education Board, with a non-technical department teaching mathematics, science, languages and social subjects to all.

CT

Civil Technology

সিভিল টেকনোলজি

Plan, measure and build. Students learn surveying, structural and architectural drawing, construction materials, estimating and costing, and site supervision.

  • Surveying
  • Materials testing
  • AutoCAD
  • Estimating

Careers: Construction firms, LGED, PWD, consulting offices

200 seats / year2 shifts · 8 semesters
ET

Electrical Technology

ইলেকট্রিক্যাল টেকনোলজি

Generation to the switchboard: electrical machines, house and industrial wiring, measurement, switchgear and distribution networks.

  • Machines lab
  • Wiring shop
  • Measurement
  • Switchgear

Careers: Power utilities, factories, REB, WZPDCL

200 seats / year2 shifts · 8 semesters
MT

Mechanical Technology

মেকানিক্যাল টেকনোলজি

Machine-shop practice, welding, foundry and forging, thermal engineering, machine design and production management for the region's factories.

  • Machine shop
  • Welding
  • Foundry
  • Thermo lab

Careers: Manufacturing plants, shipyards, jute and steel mills

200 seats / year2 shifts · 8 semesters
PT

Power Technology

পাওয়ার টেকনোলজি

Power plants, internal combustion engines, boilers, turbines and energy conversion, with maintenance practice for running plants.

  • IC engines
  • Boiler lab
  • Power plant
  • Auto shop

Careers: Power stations, automobile and marine workshops

100 seats / year2 shifts · 8 semesters
ENT

Electronics Technology

ইলেকট্রনিক্স টেকনোলজি

Analog and digital circuits, microcontrollers, communication systems and electronic maintenance, built on bench practice.

  • Circuit lab
  • Digital lab
  • Microcontroller
  • PCB

Careers: Electronics manufacturing, telecom, service centres

100 seats / year2 shifts · 8 semesters
CST

Computer Science & Technology

কম্পিউটার সায়েন্স অ্যান্ড টেকনোলজি

Programming in Python and Java, web design and development, databases, computer networking and network administration.

  • Programming
  • Networking
  • Web dev
  • Hardware

Careers: Software firms, ISPs, IT support, freelancing

100 seats / year2 shifts · 8 semesters
ENVT

Environmental Technology

এনভায়রনমেন্টাল টেকনোলজি

Water and wastewater treatment, solid-waste management, pollution monitoring and occupational safety. Formerly known as Biophysical Environment Technology.

  • Water lab
  • Waste mgmt.
  • Monitoring
  • Safety

Careers: DoE, water utilities, ETP plants, NGOs

100 seats / year2 shifts · 8 semesters
IPCT

Instrumentation & Process Control

ইন্সট্রুমেন্টেশন অ্যান্ড প্রসেস কন্ট্রোল

Sensors, transducers, calibration, PLCs and process control loops used to run modern industrial plants.

  • PLC lab
  • Calibration
  • Sensors
  • SCADA

Careers: Fertiliser, cement, power and process industries

100 seats / year2 shifts · 8 semesters
RAC

Refrigeration & Air Conditioning

রেফ্রিজারেশন অ্যান্ড এয়ার কন্ডিশনিং

Refrigeration cycles, cold storage, HVAC design, installation and servicing for homes, industry and the fish-processing sector.

  • RAC shop
  • HVAC
  • Cold storage
  • Brazing

Careers: HVAC contractors, cold storages, fish processing

100 seats / year2 shifts · 8 semesters
