Session 2026–27

Start your Diploma in Engineering at KPI.

Admission to the first semester is handled by the Bangladesh Technical Education Board (BTEB) through its central online system. Seats are allotted on merit from SSC results.

Apply on BTEB portal Admission notices
The process

Four steps to your seat

Dates, minimum GPA and fees change each year. Always follow the latest BTEB circular and the institute notice board.

Check eligibility

Pass SSC, Dakhil, SSC (Vocational) or an equivalent exam and meet the GPA requirement in the current circular.

Apply online

Fill the form on btebadmission.gov.bd, choose institutes, technologies and shifts in order of preference, and pay the fee by mobile banking.

Check the merit list

BTEB publishes selection results in phases. Check your allotted institute, technology and shift with your application ID.

Confirm at KPI

Come to the institute with your documents, verify them, pay the admission fee and collect your ID and class routine.

Documents

Bring these on admission day

  • Printed BTEB application form and selection copy
  • SSC / equivalent mark sheet and testimonial (original + photocopies)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Birth registration certificate or NID copy
  • Guardian's NID copy
  • Admission fee payment receipt

The final list of documents is given in each year's admission notice from the institute.

FAQ

Common questions

Have a question that isn't here? Contact the office

Can I apply directly at the institute?
No. First-semester applications for all government polytechnics go through the BTEB online portal. After selection, you complete admission in person at KPI.
What is the difference between the 1st and 2nd shift?
The curriculum, teachers and certificate are the same. Only the class timing differs: the 1st shift runs in the morning and the 2nd shift in the day.
How do I choose a technology?
Look at what you enjoy and the job market around you. Civil, Electrical and Mechanical have the most seats. Computer and Electronics suit students who like programming and circuits. You can compare all nine on the technologies page.
Is there admission in later semesters?
Readmission and fee notices for continuing students (for example 3rd and 4th semester) are posted on the notice board each term.
Can I continue to a B.Sc. after the diploma?
Yes. Diploma engineers can sit for admission to B.Sc. in Engineering programmes at DUET and other universities that accept diploma holders.
