About KPI

Six decades of hands-on engineering education.

Khulna Polytechnic Institute is a government institute under the Directorate of Technical Education, Technical and Madrasah Education Division, Ministry of Education.

At a glance

Who we are

KPI was established in 1963 with one aim: to build skilled human resources for the country. It is now one of the largest and oldest polytechnics in Bangladesh, teaching the four-year Diploma in Engineering in nine technologies.

The institute sits on 32.25 acres in the Khalishpur industrial area, about 4.5 km from Khulna railway station and the divisional bus terminal. Each year around 1,200 students join across nine technologies, and about 4,500 students study here at any time.

Khulna is an industrial region, so our technologies (Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Power, Electronics, Computer, Environmental, IPCT and RAC) are chosen to match the industries around us. We keep close links with employers and share final-semester student databases with them.

History

How KPI came to be

  1. 1955–60

    Work on a technical training centre in Khulna begins under Pakistan's First Five-Year Plan.

  2. 1963

    Khulna Polytechnic Institute is formally established to build skilled technical manpower for the region.

  3. 1971 onward

    After independence, KPI becomes part of Bangladesh's national technical education system under the Directorate of Technical Education.

  4. Today

    Nine technologies, two shifts, about 1,200 new students a year, and a BTEB Registered Training Organisation.

Mission

What we work towards

Our goals, taken from the institute's own statement of purpose.

Quality technical education

Teach the BTEB diploma curriculum with balanced theory and workshop practice in every semester.

Skilled human resources

Prepare diploma engineers who can work in industry at home and abroad and help Bangladesh grow.

Industry linkage

Work closely with industries in the Khulna region to arrange attachments and jobs for graduates.

Administration

Our teachers run theory and practical classes on a regular schedule and assess students carefully, so that every student from this region becomes a capable diploma engineer.
