About the institute

One of the oldest and largest polytechnics in the country.

Khulna Polytechnic Institute was founded in 1963 to create skilled technical manpower for the region. Planning for a technical training centre here began during Pakistan's First Five-Year Plan (1955–60), and today the institute runs under the Technical and Madrasah Education Division of the Ministry of Education.

Because Khulna is an industrial region, every one of our nine technologies is chosen to match the needs of local industry. We keep close links with employers and share final-semester student databases with them for recruitment.

  • Registered Training Organisation (RTO) of BTEB
  • Two shifts, morning and day
  • 4.5 km from Khulna rail station
  • BNCC, Rover Scout & Red Crescent units

Admission

From SSC result to your first workshop class.

Admission to all government polytechnics is handled centrally by the Bangladesh Technical Education Board through its online portal.

Check eligibility

Pass SSC, Dakhil or SSC (Vocational) and meet the minimum GPA in the current BTEB circular.

Apply online

Submit your application and technology choices on btebadmission.gov.bd and pay the fee.

Merit selection

Seats are allotted on SSC results. Check your institute, technology and shift online.

Confirm at KPI

Bring your documents to the institute, pay the admission fee and collect your class routine.
